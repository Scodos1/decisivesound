Privacy Policy

This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our services. Please read it carefully before making a booking.

person
1. Information We Collect

When you make a booking, we collect your name, phone number, email address, event details (date, location, expected attendance), and payment information processed securely through Paystack. We also collect any messages you send us via WhatsApp or our contact forms.

usage
2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to process bookings, send booking confirmations and updates, communicate with you about your event, handle refunds, and improve our services. We may also send you occasional promotional messages, which you can opt out of at any time.

share
3. Information Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. We only share your data with Paystack for payment processing, our delivery team for logistics, and when required by law or legal processes.

lock
4. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information. Payment processing is handled entirely by Paystack, which uses industry-standard encryption. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

schedule
5. Data Retention

We keep your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide our services. You can request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

verified
6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information. You can also opt out of marketing communications at any time. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below.

cookie
7. Cookies and Tracking

We do not use cookies or any tracking technologies on our website. Your browsing experience with us remains private.

edit
8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page, and the effective date will be updated accordingly.
